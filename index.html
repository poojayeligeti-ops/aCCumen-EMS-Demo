Who does what in aCCumen EMS

The Admin gives each person one or more roles in Users and Roles, and a role decides which modules that person can open. The roles work across three connected products: EMS, aCCumen Colony Counter 1000 and the aCCumen Identifier. Pick a screen below. It opens already signed in as that role's demo user.

How a sample moves through the roles

  1. QC→
  2. AI MachineorObserver→
  3. Reviewer→
  4. Approver→
  5. Identifier→
  6. QA Reviewer→
  7. Head of QA

Groups with aCCumen 1000 images are counted by the AI and go straight to the Reviewer. Groups with no machine image go to the Observer for a manual count first.

Head of QA

Setup · ReleaseOpens in EMS

Head of QA. Sets up site data, sampling plans and PMS. In Product Batch Release, reviews the deviation checklist for each batch: ticks every record they accept, sends weak ones back to QA with a mandatory comment and e-signature, and uses Approve & Release Batch once every record is approved.

QA Reviewer

Setup · Batch reviewOpens in EMS

QA Reviewer. Sets up site data, sampling plans and PMS. In Product Batch Summary, fills one deviation record per deviation — what happened (auto-filled, plus a root cause) and how it is fixed (immediate and preventive action, optional attachments) — then submits the batch to Head of QA. Returned records come back with the Head of QA’s comment; revising saves a new version and keeps the old one. An Approver’s at-risk decision from Colony Counter pre-fills its record.

Observer

CountingNewOpens in aCCumen Colony Counter

Counts dishes that have no machine image. Acquires manual-count groups, such as GRP-1007-14, from the Observer pool. Uploads a photo of each dish, marks every colony, then e-signs Send for Review.

Reviewer

Sign-offOpens in aCCumen Colony Counter

Checks each dish's count against its image, whether it came from the AI or from the Observer's photo. Corrects a count with a reason if needed, then sends it for approval.

Approver

Sign-offOpens in aCCumen Colony Counter

Signs off reviewed counts, approves at risk with a required justification and CAPA upload, rejects a sample, or returns counts to the Reviewer with a comment. An at-risk CAPA goes to QA in EMS for acceptance.
